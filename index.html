Panduan Sudut Berelasi Kuadran I - IV

Gunakan patokan 180° / 360° (fungsi tetap) atau 90° / 270° (fungsi berubah).

Kuadran I (0° - 90°)

Semua Positif (+)

  • sin(90° - α) = cos α
  • cos(90° - α) = sin α
  • tan(90° - α) = cot α
Kuadran II (90° - 180°)

Hanya SIN & CSC Positif (+)

  • sin(180° - α) = sin α
  • cos(180° - α) = -cos α
  • tan(180° - α) = -tan α
  • sin(90° + α) = cos α
Kuadran III (180° - 270°)

Hanya TAN & COT Positif (+)

  • sin(180° + α) = -sin α
  • cos(180° + α) = -cos α
  • tan(180° + α) = tan α
  • cos(270° - α) = -sin α
Kuadran IV (270° - 360°)

Hanya COS & SEC Positif (+)

  • sin(360° - α) = -sin α
  • cos(360° - α) = cos α
  • tan(360° - α) = -tan α
  • cos(-α) = cos α
Tips Hafalan Kuadran: "Semua Sinus Tangen Cosinus" (Kuadran I → II → III → IV yang bernilai positif).
